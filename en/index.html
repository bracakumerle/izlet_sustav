About

iZLET are Braća Kumerle — a Croatian rock band connecting the energy of live performance with an original sound shaped between the Adriatic coast and the continental interior.

Original music through albums, singles and thematic projects rooted in Croatian cultural, historical and spiritual heritage. Active since 2007.
