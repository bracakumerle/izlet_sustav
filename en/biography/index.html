About the Band

iZLET (Braća Kumerle)

iZLET is a Croatian rock band from Zagreb, founded in 2007. The band consists of brothers Toni and Petar Kumerle, registered authors with the Croatian Music Authors' Society (HDS-ZAMP). The band also operates under the name Braća Kumerle.

Their first public performance took place on 23 March 2007 at the III. Gymnasium in Zagreb, where the band performed under the name Izlet. Since then, they have operated continuously as an original music project with a rock sound shaped between the Adriatic coast and the continental interior.

Discographic History

In 2009, the song "Ti si čudesna" was included on the compilation CMC Demo 2009, released by Croatia Records — one of Croatia's leading record labels.

The debut studio album Nikad ne znaš, to je ono… was released in 2016 by Dallas Records. The second studio album Katarza was released in 2019, also by Dallas Records.

Since 2023, the band has released singles under their own label Braća Kumerle Music, distributed via DistroKid.

Thematic Focus

iZLET's catalogue covers original themes connected to Croatian cultural and homeland heritage, spirituality and personal expression. Key singles include Krijesovi laži, Znakovlje Hrvata, Frane Tente, Sjever uz odsutne, Moj Dinamo and Isuse moj.
